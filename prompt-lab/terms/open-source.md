# The open-source notice

What you make in this lab is code: the model file -- and, for a web
app, its page -- that the agent writes and you edit.  Unless you chose
closed source before your first build, or your session is private, that
code is published as you build it, on this session's page and in the
lab's public archive, under the GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3 or later (the AGPL), the license of Gendl, which it runs on.

- Anyone may read, run, change and share it, under the same license.
- Your prompts, the agent's replies and the files you upload are
  public with the session too.
- The copyright line at the top of the file names you, or your
  session; you keep the copyright and grant the AGPL to everyone.
- A closed-source work is seen by nobody else for its closed period.
  Undecided at the end of it, it becomes public under the AGPL.
- Hosting or shipping your code closed, you need either the quarterly
  royalty report or a commercial license; the lab's Decide panel says
  how.
